### Theory

Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging is the process of assigning a part of speech (such as noun, verb, adjective) to each word in a sentence. One effective approach for POS tagging is to use a statistical model called a Hidden Markov Model (HMM).

In an HMM, we use two key matrices:
- **Emission Matrix**: Probability of a word being generated by a particular POS tag.
- **Transition Matrix**: Probability of one POS tag following another in a sequence.

The **Viterbi algorithm** is a dynamic programming technique used to find the most probable sequence of hidden states (POS tags) given a sequence of observed events (words). It efficiently computes the best path through the state space by filling out a table (the Viterbi matrix) column by column, using the emission and transition probabilities.
